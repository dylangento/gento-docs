:::danger Before installation
Power on the robot only after it is securely fixed. Do not install or run a robot that is damaged or missing parts. Put a safety fence around the robot and clearly mark its motion area.
:::

## Unpacking check

On delivery, check the contents against the packing list. When installing several robots, confirm the robot and controller serial numbers match; a mismatch can cause unexpected motion.

## Handling

- The Marvin arms weigh 16–26 kg in total, including packaging and excluding the base. Lift them with several people; do not carry them alone.
- The robot base weighs about 130 kg. Do not lift it by hand; use proper handling equipment.
- Before long-distance transport, return the robot to its packing posture and use the dedicated carton.
- Avoid excessive vibration and shock. Crane, forklift, and similar work must be done by qualified or authorized personnel.

## Storage and environment

Store at -10 to 60 °C without frost. The installation site must be:

- Indoors, out of direct sunlight
- Away from dust, oil mist, salt, metal chips, and flammable or corrosive liquids and gases
- 0 to 45 °C during operation, 20 to 80 %RH (non-condensing)
- Vibration at or below 4.9 m/s² (0.5 G)
- Away from strong magnetic fields, electromagnetic sources (such as TIG welders), and electrical noise
- Below 1000 m altitude

After a long stop near 0 °C, the drives may be stiff and raise an alarm at start-up. If so, warm up at 25% speed for 10 minutes.

## Zero posture and zero calibration

Before use, confirm the robot's zero posture matches Figure 2-1 of the manual. If it does not, contact Gento support.

Zero calibration is done at the factory and is not needed in daily use. Recalibrate after:

- Replacing a joint driver or a whole joint
- Reinstalling the system or replacing the controller drive
- A severe collision

Calibration positions for each axis are in Figure 2-2 of the manual.

## Fixing the arms

Each arm base has 11 mounting holes. Recommended fasteners:

| Item | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Screw | Hex socket head cap screw M4×14 (GB/T 3098.1-2010, grade 12.9) |
| Washer | Plain conical spring washer (JIS B 1251-2018 2L-4) |
| Tightening torque | 4.8 N·m |
| Quantity | 11 |

- The mounting surface must be flat to 0.05 mm or better; otherwise the base may be damaged or performance reduced.
- Align the notch on the back of the robot base with the notch on the back of the mounting bracket.
- The foundation must carry the static load and the reaction forces from arm motion.
- With the arm fully extended, the wrist tool or workpiece must not touch walls or the fence.
- Locating bolts, dowel pins, base plates, and other mounting parts are supplied by the customer.
- The series can be mounted at any angle with the same fixing method. For wall or ceiling mounting, contact Gento first and add fall protection.
- M3-SRS/CCS and M6-SRS/CCS share the same base and mounting method.

Base mounting dimensions for M6 and M3 are in Figures 2-3 and 2-4 of the manual.

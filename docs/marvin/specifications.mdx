## Range of motion

| Joint | M6-CCS | M6-SRS | M3-CCS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| J1 (rotation) | ±178° | ±178° | ±178° |
| J2 (lower arm) | ±120° | ±120° | ±120° |
| J3 (upper arm) | ±178° | ±178° | ±178° |
| J4 (wrist rotation) | -145° to +60° | -145° to +60° | -145° to +60° |
| J5 (wrist swing) | ±178° | ±178° | ±178° |
| J6 (wrist turn) | ±60° | ±110° | ±60° |
| J7 (wrist turn) | ±90° | ±360° | ±90° |

All three models have a maximum speed of 3.14 rad/s (180°/s) on J1–J7. On CCS models, the J6 and J7 ranges are coupled; see Figure 4-4 of the manual.

## Differences between sources

The product brochure (June 2026) differs from the manual as follows. Follow the manual and confirm against your delivered configuration:

| Item | Manual V2.2 | Product brochure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arm supply | DC48V ±20%; per arm 300 W typical, 600 W peak | 48 V (±10%); 400 W average, 600 W peak |
| M6-SRS repeatability | ±0.02 mm | ±0.03 mm |
| M6-SRS J7 range | ±360° | ±178° |

Tool flange dimensions, payload center-of-gravity limits, and mounting dimensions are in sections 4.6, 4.7, and 2.5 of the manual.

:::note Source difference
The July 2026 brochure labels JB4 as side swing, JB5 as pitch, and JB7 as pitch, which differs from the manual. Confirm joint definitions against the delivered robot's URDF and controller configuration before planning motion.
:::

## Electrical and communication

- Operating voltage: LiFePO4 battery DC48V 34.4Ah, operating range DC 36–60 V
- External communication: WiFi / Ethernet
- Internal bus: 1 kHz EtherCAT real-time bus
- Encoders: all rotary joints of the arms and torso have inner and outer encoders

## Operating environment

- -10 to 40 °C ambient, below 90 %RH (non-condensing)
- Indoors on flat, solid, non-slip floors
- No rain, snow, standing water, corrosive, flammable, explosive, or very dusty environments

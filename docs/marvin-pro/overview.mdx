Marvin Pro is a fixed-base dual-arm platform for manipulation, teleoperation, and data collection. Its two seven-joint arms provide 14 degrees of freedom, excluding end effectors. The torso and head are fixed.

![Marvin Pro dual-arm platform in green, blue, and gray](/img/marvin-pro/colors.webp)

## System components

![Marvin Pro on its fixed stand with the integrated cabinet](/img/marvin-pro/product.webp)

| Component | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Two force-controlled arms | Position and impedance-controlled manipulation |
| Fixed stand and integrated electrical cabinet | Mechanical support, power, and connections |
| Head and wrist cameras | Environment and close-range manipulation views |
| Robot-side compute | Apex services, camera capture, and recording, depending on configuration |
| Desktop application and VR headset | Setup, operator control, and teleoperation |

The brochure describes an M6 configuration with a 6 kg rated payload per arm and a 696 mm working radius, and an M6 Lite configuration with 5 kg and 615 mm. These are different configurations, not interchangeable specifications.

Gento Skye is a wheeled lifting humanoid robot. Two force-controlled Marvin arms, a 2-DoF waist, and a head sit on a lifting column, on an omnidirectional chassis with four-wheel drive and four steering wheels. It is a development platform for embodied AI, reinforcement learning, and real-world validation, with whole-body force control as standard and open control access. Motion control and planning (the "cerebellum") come ready, so you can focus on models and algorithms.

![Gento Skye wheeled lifting robot](/img/skye/hero.webp)

The lifting column moves the upper body through a 785–1685 mm lifting range, so Skye can work from floor level up to about 2 m.

## Degrees of freedom

| Section | DoF |
| --- | --- |
| Head | 2 (pan and tilt) |
| Arms | 7 × 2 |
| Waist | 2 |
| Lifting column | 1 |
| Chassis | 4 |
| Total | 23 (excluding end effectors) |

## Main components

| Component | Role |
| --- | --- |
| NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin 64GB (standard) or Jetson Thor T5000 (optional) | AI computing (the "brain"): perception and model inference; runs the teleoperation back end |
| x86 motion controller | Motion control (the "cerebellum") for the arms, waist, lifting column, and head |
| Marvin M6S CCS-696 arms | 7-DoF force-controlled arms, 6 kg rated payload each. Other Marvin arms are available as options |
| Head | RealSense D435 RGB-D camera and two SHW5G cameras, stereo speakers, 4-mic array, and RGB light strip |
| Wrist cameras | SHW5G monocular (standard) or RealSense D405GA depth (optional) |
| Linghou TX-S2 chassis | Omnidirectional drive and 3D SLAM navigation, with diagonally mounted 3D LiDARs and ultrasonic sensors |
| LiFePO4 battery, DC48V 34.4Ah | Robot power; manual charging, automatic charging, or quick battery swap |

### Arm options

| Arm | Rated payload per arm | Fitment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marvin M6S CCS-696 | 6 kg | Standard |
| Marvin M6S CCS-809 | 5 kg | Optional |
| Marvin M3S Long CCS-920 | 3 kg | Optional |
| Marvin M12 CCS-805 | 12 kg | Optional |
| Marvin M20S CCS-1050 | 20 kg | Optional |

The arm values in these pages are for the standard M6S CCS-696.

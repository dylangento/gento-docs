## Daily inspection

| When | Check |
| --- | --- |
| Before power-on | The body has no knocks or deformation; cables aren't frayed, kinked, or pinched; the E-stop button springs back when pressed |
| During operation | No metallic rubbing noise, no irregular shaking, no abnormal heat from the motors or battery |
| After shutdown | Clean dust and debris off the body, return the arms to the storage position, power off, and disconnect the battery compartment power |

## Periodic maintenance

| Task | Interval | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exterior cleaning | Weekly | Dust with a dry cloth or brush. Never wash the shell with water or cleaning agents |
| Fasteners | Monthly | Check the chassis and joint fixing screws; tighten loose ones to the standard torque |
| Joint lubrication | Quarterly | Apply the manufacturer's original grease to the mechanical joints. Never mix in other grease |
| Zero-point calibration | Every 3 months | Complete a teach zero-point calibration |
| Leveling and repeatability | Yearly | Re-check the robot's leveling and repeatability |

## Maintenance rules

1. Service the robot regularly following Gento's maintenance recommendations.
2. If a fault develops during operation, stop the robot immediately and contact qualified technical staff.
3. Never maintain the robot body while the robot is running.
4. Before maintaining the body, press the E-stop so the robot can't move.
5. Before any maintenance that involves electrical circuits, power off the robot.
6. Update the robot's software promptly to avoid security risks.
7. Use only accessories specified by the manufacturer when replacing parts.
8. Don't disassemble the product or modify its design or configuration without the manufacturer's authorization.
9. Don't service the sensors or the control system yourself.
10. Charge the battery only with the original charger, within the operating temperature range, and away from excessive humidity.
11. Before scrapping the robot, remove the battery and recycle or dispose of it safely and in line with regulations.

## Safety during use

1. Operate within the rated payload; never overload the robot.
2. Use end effectors correctly, to avoid damage.
3. Use the robot on level ground. On slopes, keep the gradient at 10° or less.
4. Only qualified personnel may disable the robot's active safety functions.
5. In an emergency, press the E-stop on the robot immediately.
6. Don't interfere with the robot while it is moving.
7. Don't touch areas where body parts can be pinched, such as the armpits, the inside of the elbows, and the front of the knee joints.
8. Keep your feet away from the wheels when the robot drives or turns.
9. Don't stand between the robot and a nearby wall.
10. The robot is not a toy. Take extra care when children are nearby.

After use, store the robot in its designated place, never on a slope, and switch off its power if it won't be used for a long time.

## General safety rules

- **Area safety:** before operating, make sure there are no people or obstacles in the robot's working area.
- **Low-speed testing:** run new programs and changed parameters at low speed first.
- **Emergency stop:** in any emergency, press the red physical E-stop immediately. The software E-stop in GentoPlatform is only a backup for when you can't reach it.
- **Parameter backup:** back up the system parameters regularly.
- **Training:** operators must be trained and familiar with the operating procedures and safety rules.

## Transport

1. Switch off the robot before moving it.
2. For long distances, use the official flight case or wooden crate. Secure the robot with boards, foam, and straps, and avoid violent jolts. See [Repack for storage or transport](./first-use.mdx#repack-for-storage-or-transport).
3. The robot and its packaging are heavy and can pinch. Wear protective equipment and handle them with care.

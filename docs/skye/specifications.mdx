## Appearance

Pearl white full shell, PC/ABS with a matte painted finish.

## Operating environment

- -10 to 40 °C ambient (the manual's safety precautions give -10 to 50 °C), 10–90 %RH, non-condensing
- Level ground; slopes of no more than 10°
- No rain, standing water, corrosive, flammable, explosive, or very dusty environments

## Source differences

The September 2026 product sheet gives some values that differ from the V1.2 manual:

| Parameter | Manual V1.2 | Product sheet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dimensions | 670 × 599 × 1544 mm (retracted) | 670 × 548 × 1600 mm (collapsed) |
| Runtime | 4–6 h | 4 h |
| Head tilt | +35°/-15° | +35°/-25° |
| Speakers | 8 W × 2 | 4 W × 2 |
| Waist pitch | -90°/0° | +90°/0° |
| Chassis height | 386 mm | 380.5 mm |
| Orin memory | 64 GB | 32 GB / 64 GB |

Check these against your delivered robot and order configuration before you plan motion or integration.
